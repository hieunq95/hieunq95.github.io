I am a Senior Wireless Engineer at Huawei Singapore Research Center. Before that, I spent some time at Deloitte Australia developing LLM agents for knowledge retrieval. I obtained my PhD from University of Technology Sydney, Australia, where I worked with Prof. [Hoang Dinh](https://sites.google.com/view/dinh-thai-hoang/) and Prof. [Diep Nguyen](https://sites.google.com/view/diep-n-nguyen/home?authuser=0). Prior to that, I worked as a Research Engineer in Prof. [Dusit Niyato](https://personal.ntu.edu.sg/dniyato/)'s group at Nanyang Technological University, Singapore.

Research Interests
======
- Wireless communications (Wi-Fi 8 / IEEE 802.11bn, channel modelling and estimation, 5G/6G networks)
- Integrated sensing and communications (Wi-Fi sensing, wireless digital twins, human sensing)
- Machine learning and deep learning for wireless systems (deep reinforcement learning, generative AI)

Experience
======
My work experience includes wireless communications topics: wireless channel modelling and estimation, wireless sensing, and wireless QoS optimization. I am interested in using machine learning & deep learning algorithms to solve emerging problems in wireless communications.
